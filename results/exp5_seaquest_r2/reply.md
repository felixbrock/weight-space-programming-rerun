**Design**

**Strategy.** The submarine parks its two cells on columns 4 and 5. Turning left or right only swaps which cell is the front, so the columns stay fixed. Only the row changes. In each row the policy does the following:

1. **Shoot.** If an enemy is in its row, it faces the enemy and fires.
2. **Dodge.** If an enemy bullet is close in its row, or an enemy sits on its back cell, it moves to an unblocked neighbouring row.
3. **Hunt.** Otherwise it moves toward rows holding enemies or divers, weighting nearer rows more. Divers are collected simply by staying in their row while they drift into the submarine.
4. **Never enter a blocked row.** A row is blocked if a hazard is within one column of the body.
5. **Stay off rows 0 and 1.** It does not use rows 0 or 1, because a sticky "up" there could surface it with no diver, which ends the game.
6. **Surface when needed.** When it has at least one diver and oxygen is below 60, it climbs to row 0. It then dives back down at once, waiting first if row 1 is dangerous.

**Layer 1.** Each per-row unit is gated: the player cells in its row have weight 15 and the bias is about −30. So the unit equals tanh(local feature) when the player is in that row, and −1 otherwise. This makes the features relative to the player's row.
- **H_r (rows 2–8, units 0–6).** Computes 0.5 + 0.15·(E + 2·O).
  - E is enemies/bullets on the left minus those on the right.
  - O is +1 when facing left and −1 when facing right, read from the back cell in the trail channel.
  - The unit is only active when the body is exactly on columns 4 and 5.
- **U_r (rows 2–8, units 7–13) and D_r (rows 2–7, units 14–19).** Each sums three things:
  - attraction to targets above (U) or below (D);
  - a −16 block for hazards next to the body in the adjacent row;
  - a +8 threat for bullets in the current row, plus a "stay" penalty when a diver is in the current row.
- **Global units:**
  - S (unit 20): has a diver AND oxygen is below 60.
  - S1 (unit 21): S while in row 1.
  - R0 (unit 22): in row 0 with row 1 clear.
  - R1 (unit 23): in row 1 with row 2 clear.
  - Cx (unit 24): horizontal offset from the column-4/5 position.

**Layer 2.**
- **Units 0–6.** Thresholds on the H value split it into fire-left, turn-right, turn-left and fire-right zones.
- **Units 7–11.** Read the U and D values as:
  - an attraction level (7 for up, 10 for down);
  - a threat step (8 for up, 11 for down);
  - "surface and the way up is free" (9).
- **Units 12–15.** Pass through S1, the two row-0/1 dive units, the centring unit and S.

**Output logits.**
- No-op: 0.
- Fire: 2 at rest, 8 when an enemy is in line.
- Turn (left or right): 5.
- Up/down: about 4 for attraction, about 14 for a threat.
- Surfacing: up to about 12–15.
- Diving from row 0 or 1: about 6.7.
