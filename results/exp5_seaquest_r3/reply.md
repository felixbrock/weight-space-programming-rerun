**Design**

The core trick is that the submarine never moves left or right. Their logits are −30. It starts at column 5 facing left, so it stays at column 5 facing left for the whole episode. Its column and facing are therefore always known. Bullets always fly left, so anything in columns 0–4 of the sub's row can be shot. Columns 6–8 are where threats come from behind.

**Layer 1: row-gated units.** Every row-gated unit gets weight +10 from each `player` cell of its row and a bias of −20. When the sub is in that row, the unit is linear in its content. In any other row it saturates at −1. Layer 2 sums each 8-unit family, adds 0.5·R0a and +7.5, and reads off the value of the sub's own row.

- **Units 0–7, Z_s (fire/dodge, rows 1–8):**
  - +1.5 per enemy in columns 0–4, which is a fire cue.
  - −4.5 per enemy bullet in columns 2–4 (danger without reward), and −4 or −6 per danger in columns 6–8. These are dodge cues.
  - Divers to the left or right count positive, which means "stay here".
  - −1.2 per own bullet in columns 0–4, so the sub can leave once a shot is on its way.
- **Units 8–15, V_s (vertical drive):** for rows within 4 of the sub's row, enemies and divers on the left (and divers on the right) pull the sub toward them. The pull falls with distance: 1.5, 0.9, 0.6, 0.45. There is also a small bias toward the middle rows.
- **Units 16–23, Q_s (safety):** danger in columns 4–7 of the row above gives +6; the same in the row below gives −6.
- **Unit 24, R0a:** the sub is in row 0.
- **Unit 25, R0s:** the sub is in row 0 and row 1 is safe to enter.
- **Unit 26, R1:** the sub is in row 1.
- **Unit 27, SURF:** at least one diver (gauge cell (9,8) is lit) and oxygen below 60 (gauge cell (9,2) is dark).

**Layer 2 decoders:**

| Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 FIRE | target in own row, fire |
| 1 DODGE | threat in own row, leave the row |
| 2 VDIR | signed vertical drive toward targets |
| 3 SAFE_DN | row above is dangerous, don't go up |
| 4 SAFE_UP | row below is dangerous, don't go down |
| 5 SURF | surfacing needed |
| 6 BAN | in row 1 with no surfacing need, so going up would mean surfacing without a diver |
| 7, 8 | row-0 pass-throughs (R0s, R0a) |

**Output logits:**
- **Fire:** wins when there is a target and no threat.
- **Dodge:** raises both up and down, and VDIR or the safety units pick the side.
- **Otherwise:** the sub moves vertically toward targets.
- **Surfacing:** forces up unless the row above is unsafe.
- **Row 0:** the sub dives when row 1 is safe.
